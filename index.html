Was the Bus On Time?

Coming soon: unofficial on-time statistics for Intercity Transit buses.

We record Intercity Transit's public real-time data around the clock and compare each departure with the published schedule. When the site opens, it will show how often buses leave on time, by route, stop, hour and day. Intercity Transit is headquartered in Olympia, Washington, and serves a Public Transportation Benefit Area (PTBA) in Thurston County.

This is an unofficial project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Intercity Transit. For trip planning and live bus times, please use Intercity Transit's own tools.

Questions? Email contact@wasthebusontime.com.
