Which stops

Timepoints are the stops with times printed in the schedule. Drivers are expected not to leave them early. This matches how Intercity Transit measures on-time performance.

Times at stops between timepoints are estimates the schedule fills in between timepoints, not promises. A bus can be "early" there without having left a timepoint early, so expect lower on-time numbers in this view.
