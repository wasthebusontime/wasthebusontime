Which stops

Timepoints are the stops with times printed in the schedule. Drivers are expected not to leave them early. This matches how Intercity Transit measures on-time performance.

At stops between timepoints, the schedule's times are estimates, and buses aren't expected to keep to them exactly. A bus can show as early there without having left a timepoint early, so on-time numbers are lower in this view.

Every number counts departures: one bus leaving one stop on one trip. A trip that passes five timepoints counts as four departures here, because the last stop of a trip has no departure.
